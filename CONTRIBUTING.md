# Contributing

Bug reports and tested-camera reports are welcome.

Please include:

- Ubuntu/Linux distribution and version;
- `gphoto2 --version`;
- camera model and USB VID:PID;
- output from `aag-canon-diagnose`;
- whether Live View, AF Once, capture, download, and reconnect work.

Do not include camera serial numbers, personal image files, usernames, or private filesystem paths in public issues.

Support for additional camera models should only be marked **validated** after physical testing.
